# Levi's Strategic AI Transformation

## Business Problem
This project presents a strategic transformation proposal for Levi Strauss & Co. focused on evolving the company from a traditional denim category leader into a data-driven lifestyle brand. The analysis identifies three core business challenges: low cross-category product attachment, inventory risk in lifestyle expansion, and slow product development cycles relative to rapidly changing consumer trends. The objective was to design scalable, financially viable initiatives capable of improving personalization, increasing operational agility, and strengthening long-term competitive positioning.

## Work Performed
The project involved market analysis, competitive benchmarking, financial modeling, risk assessment, and strategic investment evaluation. Three coordinated initiatives were proposed: a personalized recommendation engine, AI-aided apparel design workflows, and licensed capsule collaborations centered around Formula 1 partnerships. Conservative financial projections, sensitivity analysis, implementation roadmaps, KPI frameworks, and risk mitigation strategies were developed to evaluate the operational and financial feasibility of the proposed $50M transformation program.
