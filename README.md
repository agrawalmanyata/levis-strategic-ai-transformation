# Levi's Strategic AI Transformation

## Business Problem
This project presents a strategic transformation proposal for Levi Strauss & Co. focused on evolving the company from a traditional denim category leader into a data-driven lifestyle brand. The analysis identified three major business challenges limiting this transition: low cross-category product attachment, inventory risk in expanding lifestyle categories, and slow product development cycles relative to rapidly changing consumer trends. The objective was to design scalable and financially viable initiatives capable of improving personalization, increasing operational agility, reducing inventory risk, and strengthening long-term competitive positioning.

## Work Performed
The project involved strategic market analysis, competitive benchmarking, financial modeling, operational feasibility assessment, sensitivity analysis, and AI-driven business transformation planning. Three coordinated initiatives were proposed: a personalized recommendation engine, AI-aided apparel design workflows, and licensed capsule collaborations centered around Formula 1 partnerships. The project also included conservative revenue forecasting, investment allocation modeling, risk mitigation frameworks, KPI design, and six-quarter implementation planning to evaluate the practicality and financial impact of the proposed $50M transformation program.

## Project Development Approach
This project was developed through a multi-stage strategic analytics and design thinking process. Multiple transformation hypotheses were initially explored and evaluated using operational feasibility, inventory turnover impact, logistics scalability, markdown recovery potential, and DTC contribution criteria. Several concepts — including AI localized assortment optimization, marketing attribution modeling, and virtual closet kiosks — were intentionally rejected after structured evaluation due to execution complexity, insufficient measurable ROI visibility, or operational constraints. The final recommendations were refined iteratively through benchmarking, financial modeling, sensitivity analysis, and risk assessment to ensure enterprise-scale feasibility and realistic implementation.
